Our School History

Iyunga Secondary School is a prestigious technical school located in Mbeya, Tanzania. We are committed to providing high-quality technical and academic education to develop responsible, skilled, and innovative students for a better future.

Mission Statement

To provide quality technical and academic knowledge that empowers young boys and girls to solve community challenges through innovation and technical expertise.

Vision Statement

To be a leading center of excellence in industrial technical secondary education across East Africa.
